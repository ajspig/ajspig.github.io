hi! I love learning, solving problems, trying new things, and connecting with people & computers. 

One of my favorite books is [Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World](https://davidepstein.com/range/). I want to become the generalist Epstein describes.

## Why this site

1) so I can connect with more people 

2) a living resume 

3) a place for me to write for the sake of writing and not for performative reasons 

4) creative experimentation. 

I want to be semi consistent posting here, and I'm still learning what that means for me. At the very least expect a yearly "brag" & 1-2 addtl posts. 

The "brag" posts serve a pretty specific purpose.
I forget things. I get discouraged. I want to improve my self-advocacy. I like working hard. I want to see the bigger picture (think celestially if you will). I like celebrating. I want an outside perspective.

They are inspired by [Julia Evans](https://jvns.ca/blog/brag-documents/).
I specifically retained the name "brag" for their title ***because*** it made me uncomfortable. A more tame title like "summary" or "recap" felt easier to swallow, but I want to be better at putting myself out there 🤷‍♀️
